OVERDRAW v1.7 - Deck and Random Map Playtest

Open OVERDRAW.html in a browser to play offline. Select Start Game, then Start
on the map to fight Gatekeeper. After winning, pick a highlighted connected
node. Fight and Elite give coins and card rewards. Chest offers a free card,
Shop buys or removes cards for coins, and Heal at Rest restores 20% of missing
HP (rounded up). Defeat the Boss to see your final route.

H draws, S stands, and N starts the next turn. The lower-left Deck shows
remaining draw-pile cards; Full deck shows all owned cards, each copy separately.
Fresh runs randomize map nodes and connections. Combat fits a 1366x768 laptop
viewport. See README.md for detailed rules and v1.7 updates.
AUDIO_CREDITS.txt, AUDIO_MANIFEST.json, and the Kenney license files list sources.

OVERDRAW v1.7 - 牌库与随机地图试玩版

双击 OVERDRAW.html 即可离线游玩。点击开始游戏，再选择地图起点迎战守门者。
战后沿连线选择高亮节点。战斗和精英战后可获得金币和卡牌，宝箱可免费选牌。
商店可以买牌，也可付费移除一张。点击休息界面的“回血”回复 20% 的缺失生命值，向上取整。击败首领后会显示本局走过的路线。

H 抽牌，S 停牌，N 进入下一回合。左下角 Deck 只展示剩余抽牌；“完整牌组”展示
全部已拥有的牌，同名牌逐张显示。随机新局会重新安排地图节点与连线。
战斗界面按 1366x768 笔记本屏幕优化。详细规则和 V1.7 更新内容见 README.md。
